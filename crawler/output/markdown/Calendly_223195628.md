Connect your calendar to Calendly
Learn how to connect your calendar to Calendly for better scheduling. Sync meetings, prevent double-booking, and coordinate team availability easily. The article outlines compatible calendars, connection options, and tips for seamless integration.
Help Center
/ Integrations
/ Calendar connections
Quick answer
Here’s how to connect your calendar to Calendly so your availability stays in sync and avoids double bookings:
In Calendly, go to Availability → Calendar settings.
Select + Connect calendar account and choose your calendar type (for example, Google, Outlook/Office 365, or Exchange).
Sign in to your calendar account and allow access.
Select the calendar(s) Calendly should check for conflicts, and choose where new bookings go.
Save your settings and make sure your calendar shows as connected.
Once connected, Calendly checks your calendar for busy times and adds new meetings to it. If you connect more than one calendar, choose one as your main calendar for bookings.

Calendly offers three embed options:

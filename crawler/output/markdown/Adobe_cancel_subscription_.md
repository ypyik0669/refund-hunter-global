Cancel your Adobe trial or subscription
Last updated on
Jul 24, 2026
Learn how to cancel your Adobe trial or subscription through your Adobe account.
You can cancel your subscription directly from your Adobe account if you purchased it from Adobe. For subscriptions purchased through third-party providers such as
Apple
,
Google
, or
Microsoft
, follow their specific cancellation process.
Sign in to your
Adobe account
.
In the
Plans and payment
window, select
Manage plan
for the plan you want to cancel.
You can also check all the apps and services included in your plan.
You can also check all the apps and services included in your plan.
Select
Cancel your plan
.
Manage your subscription by changing, upgrading, or cancelling your plan at any time.
Manage your subscription by changing, upgrading, or cancelling your plan at any time.
Note
You can’t cancel your plan if we're processing your payment or
if there is a problem with the payment
. Wait for 24 hours and try again.
Check your plan details and then select
Continue
to cancel.
Select the reason for cancellation, and then select
Continue
.
Review the cancellation details and then select
Confirm cancellation
.
Note
If you have more than one plan, each plan must be cancelled separately. Cancelling one plan does not cancel your other active plans. Check your
Adobe account
to review which plans are still active.
Check your email for a confirmation from Adobe or
verify the cancellation on your account page
.
What happens after you cancel
Your access to paid apps and services continues until the end of your current billing period. Your Adobe account stays active, and you keep access to
free membership benefits
. Learn more about
account access after plan cancellation
.
Reactivate within billing period
If you change your mind after cancellation and you're within the billing period, you can easily reactivate your plan from your
Adobe account
. Once you reactivate, your apps, services, and storage will be restored, and billing will continue as scheduled.
Note
If you still see a charge after cancelling,
this is usually the final charge for your current billing period.
Learn more about billing charges
.
Cancellation fee and refunds
A refund depends on when you cancel and the type of subscription you purchased. For most plans, you get a full refund if you cancel within 14 days of your initial purchase. Learn more about
refund eligibility when you cancel your plan
.
